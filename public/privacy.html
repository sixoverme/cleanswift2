CleanSwift ("we", "us", or "our") operates the CleanSwift management suite. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

We use your data to provide and improve the Service. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Google User Data

CleanSwift uses Google OAuth to authenticate users and provides a direct integration with Google Sheets to store your business data (Clients, Appointments, Invoices, Inventory).

  • Scopes: We request the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This allows CleanSwift to create a specific spreadsheet named "CleanSwift Manager Data" and manage only that file.
  • Access: CleanSwift does NOT access your private Google Drive files or other spreadsheets. We only interact with the data you create within the CleanSwift application.
  • Storage: Your business data is stored directly in your own Google Drive. CleanSwift does not maintain a secondary database of your business records on our servers.

3. Use of Data

We use the collected data for various purposes:

The security of your data is important to us. Remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
